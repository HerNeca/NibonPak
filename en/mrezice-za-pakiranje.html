Nets for Packaging Fruits and Vegetables

  • Return to: Packaging for Fresh Fruits & Vegetables

Quality, Functionality, and Best Price!

We offer you a wide range of nets in different colors and dimensions, perfect for machine and manual packaging of onions, garlic, citrus fruits, tangerines, peaches, and many other products.

Why Choose Our Net?

1. Attractive Product Appearance

The net comes in various colors (red, orange, yellow, white, blue…) that highlight the freshness and natural color of your fruits and vegetables, thereby increasing product appeal on shelves.

2. Practicality and Packaging Speed

Our net is adapted for machine packaging (in rolls of 2,000 meters), which enables fast and efficient processing of large quantities of packaged products. It is also suitable for manual packaging, making it a universal solution for all types of manufacturers and packing centers. For machine packaging, an additional advantage is the ability to place labels/declarations on each individual product.

3. Freshness Preservation

Extruded net enables optimal air circulation, thereby extending the freshness and quality of fruits and vegetables during storage and transport.

4. Cost-Effectiveness

The net has minimal impact on the final price of the packaged product, and with our most favorable price on the market, you achieve additional savings without compromising on quality.

Additional Benefits in Our Offer: Plastic Containers for Fruits and Vegetables

In addition to the net, our range also includes plastic containers from 500 - 1,000 grams, ideal for packaging peaches, tomatoes, grapes, and other products. The combination of container and net ensures strength, protection, and attractive appearance of your products.

In addition to nets and containers, we are the only ones on the market offering a machine for packaging containers in nets, labels, and copper wire for clipping.
